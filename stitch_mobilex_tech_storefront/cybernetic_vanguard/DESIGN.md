## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a forward-leaning, high-precision aesthetic engineered specifically for high-end consumer technology and flagship mobile devices. The brand evokes engineered perfection, velocity, and exclusive hardware mastery. It captures the visceral excitement of next-generation hardware launches while retaining the clarity, stability, and trust required of a high-conversion transactional powerhouse.

The visual direction merges **Modern Tech Minimalism** with calibrated **Cybernetic Accents**. It relies on deep, obsidian navy canvases overlaid with translucent smoky surfaces, razor-sharp technical type treatments, and selective bioluminescent light leaks. Luminescence is treated strictly as functional telemetry: Electric Blue drives interaction and navigational authority, while Neon Green highlights live system metrics such as inventory urgency, power efficiency ratings, and limited-run drops.

## Colors

The palette leverages high-contrast atmospheric dark layers to make premium device renders and hardware finishes stand out dramatically.

- **Foundational Surfaces**:
  - `Canvas Ground`: `#0B1120` (ultimate viewport base layer)
  - `Surface Base`: `#0F172A` (primary structural panels, sections, sidebars)
  - `Surface Elevated`: `#1E293B` (cards, dialogs, floating utility layers)
  - `Surface Interactive`: `#334155` (input wells, inactive chips, structural dividers)
  - `Border Subtle`: `rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.7)` (crisp technical definition)

- **Chromatics & Telemetry**:
  - `Primary Accent (Electric Blue)`: `#3B82F6` (default actions, interactive states, active indicators)
  - `Primary Pressed / Deep`: `#2563EB` (depressed states, gradient foundations)
  - `Secondary Accent (Neon Green)`: `#22C55E` / `#4ADE80` (stock status, active flash sales, precision specs)
  - `Neon Green Glow`: `rgba(74, 222, 128, 0.15)` to `rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.35)`

- **Typography & Data Tiers**:
  - `Text Primary`: `#FFFFFF` (headings, key product titles, currency values)
  - `Text Secondary`: `#CBD5E1` (body copy, active spec values, descriptive labels)
  - `Text Tertiary`: `#94A3B8` (metadata, out-of-stock notices, sub-labels)

## Typography

Typography establishes an industrial hierarchy tuned for immediate spec legibility, technical authority, and commercial conversion.

- **Headline Hierarchy**: Headlines employ negative tracking to feel sculpted, solid, and premium. Display sizes lead launch banners, product comparisons, and flagship reveals.
- **Pricing & Numeric Data**: Monetary data points utilize `label-numeric-lg` featuring strict tabular figures (`tnum`) to eliminate micro-shifts during real-time inventory and pricing adjustments.
- **Micro-Copy & Technical Specifications**: Spec tags, chipsets, display frequencies, and cellular bands utilize all-caps tracking on `label-spec` and `label-badge` to convey calibrated hardware telemetry.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8-point geometric scale, operating across a 12-column adaptive fluid grid engineered for desktop immersion with an outer limit max-width of 1440px.

- **Breakpoints**:
  - `Desktop Wide`: `1440px+` (12 columns, 64px outer margins, 32px gutters)
  - `Desktop Standard`: `1024px - 1439px` (12 columns, 32px outer margins, 24px gutters)
  - `Tablet`: `768px - 1023px` (8 columns, 24px outer margins, 16px gutters)
  - `Mobile Reflow`: `< 768px` (4 columns, 16px outer margins, 16px gutters)

- **Rhythm Rules**:
  - Component internals use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for spec pill padding and badge containment.
  - Form field, button, and card containers utilize `space-md` (16px) and `space-lg` (24px) padding.
  - Section-to-section transitions scale dynamically using `space-xl` (40px) up to 80px on desktop flagship views.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is achieved through **Tonal Stacking and Luminescent Backdrops**, rejecting plain drop shadows in favor of ambient light emissions from screens and accents.

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: `#0B1120` solid. Pure background surface for contrast anchoring.
- **Level 1 (Structural Surfaces)**: `#0F172A` with a 1px border of `rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.4)`. Used for content pods and sidebars.
- **Level 2 (Cards & Modules)**: `#1E293B` backed by a multi-tier shadow:
  - `0 10px 30px -10px rgba(2, 6, 23, 0.7)`
  - Edge light: 1px top highlight `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, 1px bottom/side border `rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.6)`.
- **Level 3 (Interactive Hover & Flyouts)**: Elevated `#1E293B` accompanied by a localized primary accent reflection:
  - `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(11, 17, 32, 0.9)`, `0 0 20px 0 rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.15)`.
- **Level 4 (Luminescent Badges & System Overlays)**: Glassmorphic layers (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.85)` + `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) with direct radial glows matching their intent (e.g., Neon Green `0 0 12px rgba(74, 222, 128, 0.35)`).

## Shapes

The geometric signature uses a disciplined **Soft** foundation (`roundedness: 1`). Controlled radiuses avoid overly rounded or playful aesthetics, retaining a surgical, industrial tool demeanor.

- **Base Radii (`rounded-sm` / 4px)**: Checkboxes, badges, micro spec tags, and technical metrics.
- **Component Radii (`rounded` / 6px to 8px)**: Buttons, text fields, search bars, countdown counter cells, and modal dialogs.
- **Structural Radii (`rounded-lg` / 12px)**: Product showcase cards, floating bottom action docks, and media comparison viewports.
- **Spec Capsules (`rounded-full`)**: Strictly reserved for hardware spec pill tags (e.g., "5G", "120Hz", "Snapdragon 8 Gen 3") and active stock status pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary High-Tech Action**: Electric Blue `#3B82F6` linear gradient transitioning to `#2563EB` at 180 degrees. Border: 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` inset on top. Text: `#FFFFFF`, bold, tracked slightly. Resting ambient glow: `0 0 16px rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.25)`. On hover: glow expands to `0 0 24px rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.45)`.
- **Secondary Ghost**: Surface `#1E293B` with border 1px solid `#334155`. Text: `#CBD5E1`. On hover: border transitions to `#3B82F6` with text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Telemetry Action (Pre-order / Buy Now)**: Flash neon accents using `#22C55E` background, dark text `#0B1120`, and rapid subtle outer pulse animation for flash sales.

### Product Cards
- Encased in `#1E293B` with a refined 1px border of `#334155`.
- Image presentation area features a subtle radial gradient behind the product render: `radial-gradient(circle at center, rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.08) 0%, transparent 70%)`.
- Includes a dedicated horizontal rack of specification pills directly above the pricing tier.
- Hover transition lifts card 4px on Y-axis and deepens the border highlight to `#3B82F6` at 50% opacity.

### Glowing Badges & Indicators
- **In Stock / Real-Time Urgency**: High-visibility pill container filled with `rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.12)`, border 1px solid `rgba(74, 222, 128, 0.4)`. Text: `#4ADE80`. Features an animated 6px live pulsing dot in `#22C55E`.
- **Sale & Limited Allocation**: Pill container filled with `rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.15)`, border 1px solid `#3B82F6`, text `#FFFFFF`.

### Countdown Timers
- Segmented block construction: Each unit (Hours, Minutes, Seconds) sits within an isolated `#0F172A` capsule, border 1px solid `#334155`.
- Numerals rendered in `Plus Jakarta Sans` 700 weight, `#FFFFFF`, with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`.
- Micro-labels beneath (HR, MIN, SEC) styled in `label-spec`, uppercase, color `#94A3B8`.

### Hardware Spec Pill Tags
- Compact tags: Height 24px, radius 9999px.
- Background: `rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.5)`. Text: `#CBD5E1`.
- On high-tier specs (e.g., "1TB Storage", "OLED"), tag takes an active micro-border of `rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.4)` with white typography.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs**: Background `#0F172A`, height 48px, border 1px solid `#334155`, text `#FFFFFF`, placeholder `#94A3B8`. Focus state applies a crisp border `#3B82F6` and an electric halo: `0 0 0 3px rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.2)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 18px square/circle with `#0F172A` fill and 1.5px `#334155` border. Checked state snaps to `#3B82F6` with an inner white glyph, emitting an ambient blue aura.
